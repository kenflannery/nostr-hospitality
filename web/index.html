WebAssembly Blocked

Hospitality Libre requires WebAssembly to render graphics. Safari has blocked WebAssembly, which usually happens when Lockdown Mode is enabled.

1 Tap the Page Settings menu in Safari's address bar (the aA icon or menu slider).
2 Tap Website Settings.
3 Turn Lockdown Mode: Off for this site.
4 Tap Refresh Page below.
Privacy Note: This only creates an exception for hospitalitylibre.com. Lockdown Mode remains active for all other websites and apps on your device.
